3. AI features and music recognition

Marvin and cover identification

Marvin uses Anthropic's Claude service through Dig's backend. When you send a message, we send the message, relevant conversation history, the current record or screen context, and a summary of your collecting profile. This summary can include your chosen name, musical tastes, collection counts, buying habits, purchase places, price ranges, wants and recent listening decisions. Marvin can also request relevant collection and wantlist details through its tools. Tool results and saved memories can be included in further requests to Anthropic. Marvin may use Anthropic's web search and Discogs to answer a question.

We store Marvin conversations and memories in your Dig account. Deleting a conversation does not by itself delete separately saved memories. Account deletion removes both from the active Dig account database.

Cover identification sends a resized image to Anthropic to read the artist, title and, when visible, year. The resulting text is then used to search Discogs. Dig's identification endpoint does not create a stored photo library, but the image is processed by our service providers and may remain in their systems under their applicable retention arrangements. Choose sleeve photos that do not include people or information you do not want to share.

What's playing

After you tap Listen and allow microphone access, ShazamKit processes short-lived audio on the iPhone and sends a musical fingerprint to Apple's Shazam service. Dig does not save the recording or fingerprint, or send either to Supabase or Anthropic. Listening stops when cancelled, when you leave the screen, or when the app goes into the background or is interrupted.

The identified song's artist, title and available album information are used to search Discogs for possible records. We save up to ten recent identifications, including song information, artwork/provider links, dates and any release you choose, and synchronize that history with your Dig account. Recognition does not identify a physical pressing and does not add music to your Apple Music or Shazam library. Clear recent history removes the visible entries and syncs that change; internal deletion markers can remain to prevent deleted data returning.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

We retain personal information for the purposes described in this policy. Retention depends on the type of information, whether it is still needed to provide a feature you requested, deletion or withdrawal requests, and any applicable security, transaction, dispute or legal requirements. Where a fixed period is not specified, the following criteria determine how long we keep it.

Device copies and synchronization deletion markersLocal copies support offline use; deletion markers prevent deleted records from returning during synchronization. Their handling and current limitations are explained below.

Your account data is stored on your device for offline use and synchronized with our backend. Signing out hides the account's local copy and preserves pending offline changes for that account; it does not delete the account or its data.

Use Settings → Delete account, or Account → Delete account from the subscription screen, to request permanent deletion. The implemented flow deletes your active Dig authentication record and private database records, including collection/preferences/history, conversations/memories and saved Discogs credentials. It also clears the requesting phone's active account copy and pending changes. Shared event and shop listings are not private account records.

Deleting your Dig account does not delete an independent Apple or Discogs account, cancel a subscription, or remove a separately submitted website waiting-list entry. Manage subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings. Contact our privacy address to remove a waiting-list entry.

The current automatic deletion flow does not delete previously ingested PostHog events or RevenueCat customer records. Signing out of these services does not erase those records. Other offline devices and legacy local database archives may retain copies; account deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure of every copy or backup.

For requests covering separately retained provider records, waiting-list entries or support correspondence, email contact@digrecords.fr. Any continued retention must have an applicable purpose or legal basis; account deletion is not authorization to keep all associated records indefinitely.

6. Legal bases and international processing

Dig is intended for worldwide availability.

Where EU or UK data protection law applies, the proposed allocation is: providing requested accounts, sync and paid features under our contract with you; consent for optional processing where required, including non-essential analytics and waiting-list communications; legitimate interests in proportionate service security and support; and legal obligations for records we must retain. Device permission alone is not consent to every subsequent use or disclosure of the data.

Your information may be processed outside your country by the providers above.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit your collecting information and location, manage iOS camera/photo/microphone/location permissions, disconnect Discogs, clear recent identified songs and delete conversations or your account using the available app controls. To stop sending new Marvin messages or cover images, stop using those features; this does not erase requests already processed.

Depending on applicable law, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or a portable copy of your personal information. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and to direct marketing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact our privacy address; we may need proportionate information to verify your identity.

You may complain to your local data protection authority, including the CNIL in France or the ICO in the UK where applicable.

Dig personalizes record suggestions and Marvin responses. It is not designed to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

8. Website storage and waiting list

The website saves your chosen language in browser local storage. Its listening demonstration requests music information and previews from Apple. Joining the waiting list supplies an email and language preference for the requested launch notification; it does not automatically create a Dig account or authorize an ongoing marketing newsletter.

9. Children and policy changes

We will update this policy when our processing changes and show the revised effective date. Where required, we will notify you of material changes and obtain any necessary new consent before introducing the relevant processing.
